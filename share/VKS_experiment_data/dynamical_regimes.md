# Dynamical regimes

Source papers: `Ravelet2008`, `Monchaux2009`.

## Regime map in |Theta|

| Regime | |Theta| range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stat1 | 0.00-0.09 | Stationary, supercritical threshold around Rm = 31 |
| Limit cycle | 0.09-0.11 | Dipole-quadrupole interaction |
| Stat2 | 0.11-0.13 | Stationary, hysteretic behavior |
| Reversals | 0.15-0.18 | Random reversals; temperature affects periodicity |
| Bursts/extinctions | 0.18-0.22 | Bursts exceed 100 G |
| Stat3 | 0.20-0.40 | Slow oscillations; amplitude extrema at 0.27 and 0.37 |
| Oscillatory | 0.40-0.60 | Threshold Theta_o approx. 0.41 |
| Stat4 | 1.00 | Single-impeller dynamo |
